
SATISFIED REQUIREMENTS
REQ-01: solution provide professionals an assistance tool accessible from their work environment (priority: MUST) (category: Professional Assistance)
REQ-02: solution retrieve information from accounting standards (PCG) (priority: MUST) (category: Information Search)
REQ-03: solution retrieve information from tax doctrine and the tax code (CGI) (priority: MUST) (category: Information Search)
REQ-04: solution retrieve information from the commercial code and applicable legal texts (priority: MUST) (category: Information Search)
REQ-05: solution retrieve information from collective-bargaining agreements (payroll/social) (priority: MUST) (category: Information Search)
REQ-06: solution retrieve information from internal procedures and methodology guides (priority: MUST) (category: Information Search)
REQ-07: solution contextualize answers to the professional's request (priority: MUST) (category: Information Consultation)
REQ-08: solution assist drafting of client letters and correspondence (priority: MUST) (category: Drafting)
REQ-09: solution assist drafting of mission notes, file memos and syntheses (priority: MUST) (category: Drafting)
REQ-10: solution surface relevant information during client-file handling (priority: MUST) (category: Client-File Handling)
REQ-11: solution recall applicable rules during client-file handling (priority: MUST) (category: Client-File Handling)
REQ-12: solution propose a checklist of verifications to perform (priority: MUST) (category: Client-File Handling)
REQ-13: solution flag potential anomalies for human review (révision des comptes) (priority: MUST) (category: Client-File Handling)
REQ-16: solution provide source grounding and citations for each generated answer (priority: MUST) (category: Explainability)
REQ-17: solution integrate with accounting-production and payroll software (priority: MUST) (category: Integration)
REQ-18: solution integrate with document management (GED) and the e-invoicing environment (priority: MUST) (category: Integration)
REQ-21: outputs be adapted to the professional context and quality standards (priority: MUST) (category: Service Quality)
REQ-23: solution reduce processing and documentation times (priority: MUST) (category: Efficiency)
REQ-24: solution provide a high level of answer relevance (priority: MUST) (category: Service Quality)
REQ-25: every figure or factual statement be grounded in and traceable to a source document, with no ungrounded numeric output (priority: MUST) (category: Reliability)

SATISFIED CONSTRAINTS
CON-01: holder must guarantee strict compliance with GDPR for client and employee data (Regulatory)
CON-02: holder must preserve professional secrecy (secret professionnel) (Regulatory)
CON-03: data must be hosted exclusively within the European Union, with no transfer outside the EU authorized (Sovereignty)
CON-05: outputs must be explainable and auditable a posteriori (audit trail) (Regulatory)
CON-07: solution must not undermine anti-money-laundering obligations (LCB-FT) (Regulatory)
CON-08: holder must comply with legal retention and archival obligations for accounting documents (Regulatory)
CON-09: client files must be strictly partitioned with no cross-client data leakage (Technical)
CON-10: sensitive data must be protected with encryption and pseudonymisation where relevant (Technical)
CON-11: hosting must be realized on infrastructure of SecNumCloud type or equivalent (Sovereignty)
CON-12: solution must minimise dependency on non-European suppliers (Sovereignty)
CON-14: solution must sustain performance during seasonal peaks and high availability during business hours (Technical)
CON-15: solution must integrate with accounting-production and payroll software (Technical)
CON-16: solution must integrate with document management (GED) (Technical)
CON-17: solution must integrate with the e-invoicing / dematerialisation environment (Technical)
CON-20: global budget is limited to 220,000 euros over 3 years (Budgetary)
CON-21: annual operating cost must remain controlled and compatible with budgetary capacities (Budgetary)
CON-22: solution must contribute to reduction of energy consumption (Environmental)
CON-23: solution must rely on low-carbon infrastructure as far as possible (Environmental)
