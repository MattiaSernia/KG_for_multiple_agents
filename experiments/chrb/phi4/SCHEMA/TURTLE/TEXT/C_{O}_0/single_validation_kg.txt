
SATISFIED REQUIREMENTS
REQ-01: solution provide staff an assistance tool accessible from their work environment (priority: MUST) (category: Staff Assistance)
REQ-05: solution retrieve information from internal documents (guides, notes, frameworks, drug references) (priority: MUST) (category: Information Search)
REQ-07: solution assist drafting of discharge summaries and medical reports (comptes-rendus) (priority: MUST) (category: Drafting)
REQ-08: solution assist drafting of medical correspondence and patient letters (priority: MUST) (category: Drafting)
REQ-09: solution assist drafting of administrative documents and dossier syntheses (priority: MUST) (category: Drafting)
REQ-10: solution surface relevant information during dossier handling (priority: MUST) (category: Dossier Handling)
REQ-14: solution support continuity of care and handover between shifts and services (priority: MUST) (category: Collaboration)
REQ-17: solution integrate with the hospital information system (SIH) and the electronic patient record (DPI) (priority: MUST) (category: Integration)
REQ-18: solution expose API-type interfaces (priority: MUST) (category: Integration)
REQ-20: generated content be adapted to clinical/administrative context and expected quality standards (priority: MUST) (category: Service Quality)
REQ-21: solution fit into existing staff practices without introducing a rupture in clinical or administrative workflows (priority: MUST) (category: Usability)
REQ-22: solution reduce processing and documentation times (priority: MUST) (category: Efficiency)

SATISFIED CONSTRAINTS
CON-01: holder must guarantee strict compliance with GDPR (Regulatory)
CON-02: holder must process health data as special-category data (GDPR Art. 9) (Regulatory)
CON-03: holder must preserve medical confidentiality (secret médical) (Regulatory)
CON-04: data must be hosted exclusively within the European Union, with no transfer outside the EU authorized (Sovereignty)
CON-05: no clinical or medico-administrative decision shall be automated without explicit validation by a qualified professional (Regulatory)
CON-06: outputs must be explainable and auditable a posteriori (HAS guidance) (Regulatory)
CON-08: hosting infrastructure must hold HDS (Hébergeur de Données de Santé) certification (Regulatory)
CON-09: sensitive data must be protected with encryption and pseudonymisation where relevant (Technical)
CON-10: hosting must be realized on infrastructure of SecNumCloud type or equivalent (Sovereignty)
CON-11: solution must minimise dependency on non-European suppliers (Sovereignty)
CON-12: response time must be lower than 2 seconds for common queries (Technical)
CON-13: solution must guarantee availability of 99.9%, including 24/7 (Technical)
CON-14: solution must integrate with the hospital information system (SIH) and the electronic patient record (DPI) (Technical)
CON-15: solution must support HL7 / FHIR interoperability standards (Technical)
CON-18: interfaces must be of type API (Technical)
CON-20: global budget is limited to 900,000 euros over 5 years (Budgetary)
CON-21: annual operating cost must remain controlled and compatible with budgetary capacities (Budgetary)
CON-22: solution must contribute to reduction of energy consumption (Environmental)
CON-23: solution must rely on low-carbon infrastructure as far as possible (Environmental)
